Revis Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Revis mobile application and its related services (the "App") handle your information. The App is operated by NouSphere Labs ("we", "us" or the "Service Provider").

In short
  • We do not require an account. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number to use the App.
  • Before your first swap, the App asks for your permission to send the photos you choose to our AI processing partner, WaveSpeed AI. Photos are used only to create your face swap. We do not keep copies of your photos on our servers.
  • Your results and history are stored on your device.
  • We use privacy-friendly product analytics (PostHog, EU servers) that is not linked to your identity. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information we process

Photos and videos you select

When you create a face swap, the photo containing your face and the selected template (a photo or video from our catalog, or a photo you choose) are sent over an encrypted connection to our server and then to our AI processing partner, WaveSpeed AI, which generates the result. These images are used only to produce the result you asked for.

The App asks for your explicit permission before your first swap and only sends photos after you agree. If you do not agree, no photos leave your device. To withdraw your permission, stop creating swaps and delete the App, or contact us.

We do not use your photos to identify you, we do not build facial recognition profiles, and we do not use your photos to train AI models.

Results and history

Finished face swaps and your swap history are saved on your device. You can delete them in the App at any time. Deleting the App also removes them, unless you have saved them to your photo library.

Device and security information

To protect the service from abuse, the App uses Apple's App Attest to confirm that requests come from a genuine copy of the App. This creates a random key identifier for your installation that does not reveal who you are. We also keep short-lived records of each swap job (a job ID, its status and the installation identifier) so that only your device can retrieve its own results.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment card details. The App sends Apple's transaction information to our server to confirm whether you have an active Revis Pro subscription.

Free usage counter

The number of free swaps you have left is stored in your device's Keychain.

Analytics

The App uses PostHog, hosted in the European Union, to understand how the App is used. It collects usage events such as features used, swaps started and completed, subscription events and content reports, together with the app version, device model and operating system. Events are tied to a random identifier created by the App, not to your name, email or photos, and are not linked to your identity. Like any internet service, PostHog receives your device's IP address when events are sent; we do not use it to identify you. We do not use analytics data for advertising and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, so the App does not show the iOS tracking prompt.

We process analytics data on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the App working and improving it. You can object at any time by contacting us.

Content reports

If you report a result in the App, we receive the reason you selected and whether it was a photo or a video. The reported image or video itself is not sent to us. If you choose to email us more details, we receive your email as described below.

Messages you send us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the content of your message, and we use them only to reply to you.

2. How we use information

  • To create the face swaps you request and deliver the results to your device.
  • To provide and verify Revis Pro subscriptions.
  • To protect the App and its users from fraud and abuse.
  • To understand how the App is used and improve it.
  • To review reports of misuse and keep the App safe.
  • To respond to your support requests and to comply with legal obligations.

4. Data retention

  • Photos and videos you select: not stored on our servers after processing. WaveSpeed AI keeps them only as described in its own policy.
  • Swap job records: deleted automatically within 7 days.
  • App Attest installation records: kept while needed to protect the service.
  • Analytics data: kept for up to 12 months.
  • Content reports: kept as long as needed to review the report and protect the App.
  • Support emails: kept as long as needed to resolve your request.

5. International transfers

Our servers are located in the European Union. Some of our providers may process data in other countries, including the United States. Where required by law, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Residents of California have the right to know, delete and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and the right to non-discrimination for exercising these rights. We do not sell personal information.

Because the App does not use accounts, most data is stored only on your device and you can delete it yourself. To make a request, contact us at saygivere@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13, or a higher age where required by local law. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections. Our API keys and processing partners are accessed only from our servers, never from the App. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information. If a data breach affects your personal data, we will notify you as required by law.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page with a new effective date and, where required by law, ask for your consent to material changes.
