Revis Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Revis mobile application and its related services (the "App") collect, use and share information. The App is operated by NouSphere Labs, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("NouSphere Labs", "we", "us" or "our"). If you have questions, contact us at info@nouspherelabs.com.

In short
  • We do not require an account. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number to use the App.
  • Before your first swap, the App asks for your permission to send the photos you choose to our AI processing partners, LightX for photo swaps and WaveSpeed AI for video swaps (see section 3). Photos are used only to create your face swap, and we do not keep copies on our own servers. For video swaps, we delete your photos and the result from WaveSpeed AI as soon as the result is on your device, and in any case within 7 days. LightX does not let us request deletion, so photos sent for photo swaps are kept and deleted under LightX's own privacy policy.
  • We do not collect, keep or sell biometric identifiers, we do not identify you from your face, and we do not use your photos to train AI models.
  • To keep the App safe and working, photos are automatically checked for nudity, sexual, graphic violent and hateful content, and to confirm that a face is visible.
  • Your results and history are stored on your device.
  • We use privacy-friendly product analytics that is not linked to your identity. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for targeted advertising.

1. Information we process

Photos and videos you select

When you create a face swap, the photo containing your face and the selected template (a photo or video from our catalog, or a photo you choose) are sent over an encrypted connection to our server and then to one of our AI processing partners, which generates the result: LightX (operated by Andor Communications Pvt. Ltd., India) for photo swaps, and WaveSpeed AI for video swaps. These images are used only to produce the result you asked for.

For video swaps, a few seconds after the App has downloaded your result, it asks our server to delete the job, including your photos and the result, from WaveSpeed AI. If that request does not go through (for example, because you closed the App or lost your connection), WaveSpeed AI deletes the job automatically within 7 days.

For photo swaps, LightX does not currently offer a way for us to request deletion of individual jobs. The photos and results it receives are kept and deleted according to LightX's privacy policy, and we cannot guarantee a specific deletion time.

The App asks for your explicit permission before your first swap and only sends photos after you agree. If you do not agree, no photos leave your device. You can withdraw your permission at any time in the App under Profile > Privacy > AI Processing Permission; the App will then ask again before sending any photo.

Automated photo checks

Before a photo is sent to an AI processing partner, our server checks it automatically using Amazon Rekognition (part of Amazon Web Services, EU region):

  • Content safety: the photos you send, and photo results before they are returned to you, are checked for nudity, sexual, graphic violent and hateful content. If a photo does not pass, it is not sent to our AI processing partner (or the result is not returned to you and, where the partner allows it, is deleted right away) and we record a counter for your installation. After repeated violations, the installation is blocked from creating swaps. You can contact us if you believe this happened by mistake.
  • Face check: the photos are checked to confirm that a face is visible and, for the photo of your face, whether glasses or sunglasses cover it, so that the App can ask you for a better photo instead of producing a poor result. This check only detects whether a face is present; it does not identify you or compare your face with any other face.

These checks run automatically, their results are not used to identify you, and the images are not stored by the checks.

Results and history

Finished face swaps and your swap history are saved on your device. You can delete them in the App at any time. Deleting the App also removes them, unless you have saved them to your photo library.

Device and security information

To protect the service from abuse, the App uses Apple's App Attest to confirm that requests come from a genuine copy of the App. This creates a random key identifier for your installation that does not reveal who you are. We also keep short-lived records of each swap job (a job ID, its status and the installation identifier) so that only your device can retrieve its own results.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment card details. We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions: the App shares Apple's transaction information (such as the product, purchase and renewal dates) with RevenueCat, together with a random identifier created by RevenueCat that is not linked to your name or email, so that your Revis Pro access can be confirmed and restored. The App also sends Apple's transaction information to our server to confirm access to Pro templates.

Free usage counter

The number of free swaps you have left is stored in your device's Keychain.

Analytics

The App uses PostHog, hosted in the European Union, to understand how the App is used. It collects usage events such as features used, swaps started and completed, subscription events and content reports, together with the app version, device model and operating system. Events are tied to a random identifier created by the App, not to your name, email or photos, and are not linked to your identity. Like any internet service, PostHog receives your device's IP address when events are sent; we do not use it to identify you. We do not use analytics data for advertising and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, so the App does not show the iOS tracking prompt.

Content reports

If you report a result in the App, we receive the reason you selected and whether it was a photo or a video. The reported image or video itself is not sent to us. If you choose to email us more details, we receive your email as described below.

Messages you send us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the content of your message, and we use them only to reply to you and to keep a record of your request.

2. How we use information

  • To create the face swaps you request and deliver the results to your device.
  • To provide and verify Revis Pro subscriptions.
  • To protect the App and its users from fraud and abuse.
  • To understand how the App is used and improve it.
  • To review reports of misuse, automatically filter objectionable content and keep the App safe.
  • To respond to your support and privacy requests and to comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your information for targeted advertising, and we do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that produce legal or similarly significant effects. The automated photo checks described above only decide whether a photo can be processed.

4. Biometric information

Creating a face swap requires analyzing the faces in the photos you choose. This analysis happens only while your swap and the automated checks described above are being processed. We do not collect, store or keep face geometry, face templates or any other biometric identifier, we do not use faces to identify or recognize anyone, and we never sell, lease, trade or otherwise profit from biometric information.

The App asks for your consent before any photo is sent for processing, and you confirm that you have the permission of everyone whose face appears in your photos. For video swaps, photos and results are deleted from WaveSpeed AI as soon as your result is delivered, and in any case within 7 days. For photo swaps, images sent to LightX are kept and deleted under LightX's privacy policy. See section 5. This section is intended to meet the requirements of laws such as the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act, the Texas Capture or Use of Biometric Identifier Act and the Washington biometric privacy law, to the extent they apply.

5. Data retention

  • Photos and videos you select, and your results: never stored on our own servers. For video swaps, deleted from WaveSpeed AI as soon as your result is on your device, and at the latest within 7 days. For photo swaps, kept and deleted by LightX under its own privacy policy, because LightX does not let us request deletion.
  • Content safety records: a count of rejected photos and, if applicable, a block flag for your installation, kept while needed to protect the service.
  • Swap job records: deleted automatically within 7 days.
  • App Attest installation records: kept while needed to protect the service.
  • Analytics data: kept for up to 12 months.
  • Subscription records at RevenueCat: kept while you have a subscription and as long as needed for accounting and to restore purchases.
  • Content reports: kept as long as needed to review the report and protect the App.
  • Support and privacy requests: kept as long as needed to resolve your request and, for privacy requests, for 24 months as required by law.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or a higher age where required by local law). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Our API keys and processing partners are accessed only from our servers, never from the App. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. If a data breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the authorities as required by law.
